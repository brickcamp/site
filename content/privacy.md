## What this site collects

BrickCamp is a static website. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking
services, and has no user accounts, comments or contact forms. Nothing you type
into the search box is transmitted anywhere — searching and filtering run
entirely in your browser against files already downloaded with the page.

Two entries may be stored on your device, in your browser's local storage: a
`theme` entry remembering whether you chose the light or dark colour scheme,
and — only if you use the [Linkbox Maker](#linkbox-maker) — a `microlink-api-key`
entry holding the key you entered there. Both are stored under § 25 (2) no. 2
TDDG as strictly necessary to provide a function you explicitly requested, which
is why this site shows no consent banner. The `theme` entry never leaves your
browser; the stored key is sent to Microlink only when you submit that tool's
form, as described below. Both can be cleared through your browser settings.

## Linkbox Maker

The [Linkbox Maker](/tools/linkbox-maker/) is an authoring helper for building
link previews. It does nothing unless you open that page and submit its form.
When you do, your browser sends the URL you entered — together with the API key,
if you supplied one — directly to the metadata service Microlink at
`api.microlink.io`, which retrieves the page behind that URL and returns its
title, description and preview image. The request goes from your browser to
Microlink; it does not pass through this site, and we neither see nor store it.

Because you trigger this transfer deliberately, the legal basis is your consent
under Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, given by submitting the form. Microlink does not
state where its servers are located, so processing outside the EU cannot be
ruled out; in that case the transfer rests on Art. 49 (1) (a) GDPR. If you would
rather not use the service, do not submit the form. Microlink's privacy policy:
<https://microlink.io/privacy>
